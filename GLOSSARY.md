# Catacomb Cats

A browser roguelite where a party of cats fights through fixed expeditions and spends the loot on permanent upgrades at the camp.

## Language

**Expedition**:
A named, fixed route of rooms with its own enemies, boss and rewards.
_Avoid_: Dungeon, level, stage, map

**Run**:
One attempt at an Expedition, from entering the first room until the party wins or is wiped.
_Avoid_: Game, session, descent

**Room**:
A single combat inside an Expedition; the last one holds the boss.
_Avoid_: Fight, battle, encounter

**Power**:
The total of permanent upgrades the player has bought: ranks of the three best cats, the Hearth rank and the mastery level.
_Avoid_: Level (alone), strength

**Ascension**:
The difficulty tier applied to every enemy in a Run, derived from Power after the first boss victory.
_Avoid_: Difficulty level, hard mode

**Rank cap**:
The highest training, Hearth and mastery level the player may reach, raised by each Expedition they unlock.
_Avoid_: Level cap, limit

**Recommended Power**:
The Power an Expedition expects the player to have before entering it; shown as a warning, never enforced.
_Avoid_: Required power, minimum level
