# Catacomb Cats

A browser roguelite where a party of cats fights through fixed expeditions and spends the loot on permanent upgrades at the camp.

## Language

**Companion**:
A playable cat of the camp; three of them make the party of a Run. The five are Miso, Luna, Pursian, Mei and Tuntun.
_Avoid_: Cat (in visible text), hero, character

**Trinket**:
A single piece of gear a Companion can wear, found as a reward in the Rooms of an Expedition; it adds one small bonus and each one can be worn by only one Companion at a time.
_Avoid_: Item, equipment

**Field Guide**:
The rules screen opened from FAQ, with a short list of frequently asked questions and one sheet per Companion showing its skills and training ranks.
_Avoid_: Help, manual, wiki

**Expedition**:
A named, fixed route of rooms with its own enemies, boss and rewards.
_Avoid_: Dungeon, level, stage, map

**Run**:
One attempt at an Expedition, from entering the first room until the party wins or is wiped.
_Avoid_: Game, session, descent

**Room**:
A single combat inside an Expedition; the last one holds the boss. The screen that shows a Room is called the Battle screen.
_Avoid_: Fight, encounter

**Power**:
The total of permanent upgrades the player has bought: ranks of the three best cats, the Hearth rank and the mastery level.
_Avoid_: Level (alone), strength

**Ascension**:
The difficulty tier applied to every enemy in a Run, derived from Power after the first boss victory.
_Avoid_: Difficulty level, hard mode

**Rank cap**:
The highest training, Hearth and mastery level the player may reach, raised by each Expedition they unlock.
_Avoid_: Level cap, limit

**Recommended Power**:
The Power an Expedition expects the player to have before entering it; shown as a warning, never enforced.
_Avoid_: Required power, minimum level

**Round**:
One full pass through the turn order of a Room; announced on screen by a banner before the enemies act.
_Avoid_: Cycle, wave

**Enemy phase**:
The stretch where enemies take their turns one after another, each attack shown on its own before play moves on.
_Avoid_: Intro attack, enemy burst

**Threat thread**:
The line drawn under the battlefield from an enemy to the cat it marked (or the enemy it will help), shown only once the Enemy phase has ended.
_Avoid_: Arrow, intent line, objective line

**Next target**:
The label hung under the start of an enemy's Threat thread that names who the thread points to, i.e. who it will act on in its following turn rather than the hit just seen.
_Avoid_: Threat objective, mark label

**Aim outline**:
The yellow outline on every unit the skill under the mouse or keyboard focus can reach. It stays hidden during the Enemy phase and the Threat thread preview, then appears after a short delay if the cursor is still resting on the skill.
_Avoid_: Target selector, highlight

**Skin**:
A color variant of a Companion, chosen at the camp; each Companion has its original look and two more, and a Skin changes only how the cat looks.
_Avoid_: Costume, outfit, palette
